# Rating Process and Criteria (NewsGuard, rating process page, 2024)

- URL: https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/
- PDF: not downloaded (web page, no PDF)
- Status: methodology page
- Why it matters for source trust: a nine-criterion, 100-point checklist applied by trained journalists, with every criterion and its weight published, so it can be copied as a rubric.

## What it says
NewsGuard rates news and information websites on nine journalistic criteria. Five criteria cover credibility and are worth 75 points. Four cover transparency and are worth 25 points. Each criterion is pass or fail; a site gets the points for every criterion it passes. The total score is a number from 0 to 100 with five labelled bands. A trained journalist analyst drafts a written "Nutrition Label" explaining each criterion. The site is contacted for comment before publication and its reply is included. At least one senior editor and the co-CEOs review every label. Satire sites and platforms get separate designations rather than scores. Ratings are updated and changes are noted with editor's notes; complaints are published.

## Method
1. Analyst reviews the site's content, corrections, ownership and staff pages.
2. Score credibility criteria (75 points): does not repeatedly publish false or egregiously misleading content, 22; gathers and presents information responsibly, 18; has effective practices for correcting errors, 12.5; handles the difference between news and opinion responsibly, 12.5; avoids deceptive headlines, 10.
3. Score transparency criteria (25 points): discloses ownership and financing, 7.5; clearly labels advertising, 7.5; reveals who is in charge including possible conflicts of interest, 5; provides names of content creators with contact or biographical information, 5.
4. Each criterion is pass or fail. A criterion marked not applicable awards its points.
5. Sum to a 0 to 100 score. Bands: 100 High Credibility; 75 to 99 Generally Credible; 60 to 74 Credible with Exceptions; 40 to 59 Proceed with Caution; 0 to 39 Proceed with Maximum Caution.
6. Contact the site for comment; include its response. Senior editor plus co-CEOs sign off. Publish, then update with editor's notes.

## Claims we can test against
1. A site that passes the two largest criteria (false content and responsible gathering, 40 points) and nothing else scores 40 and lands in Proceed with Caution.
2. Transparency alone (25 points) cannot make a site credible; credibility criteria decide the band.
3. Binary pass or fail per criterion makes scores reproducible by a second analyst; agreement rates are not published.
4. Sites with correction policies and bylines score higher on average than sites without them.
